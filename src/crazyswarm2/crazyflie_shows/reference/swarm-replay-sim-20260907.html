About this recording

What you are watching. The ring breathes, turns, runs a vertical wave, snaps into reverse, then splits into two counter‑rotating lanes that pass through each other — the counterflow, where the closest approach of the whole show happens. Clearance there is a static radial gap between concentric circles at 1.45 m and 0.48 m, not a timing coincidence, which is why it is safe on a rig with no onboard collision avoidance.

Provenance. Positions sampled from /tf at 2 kHz during the run, downsampled to 30 fps. Planned minimum separation was 0.91 m; the flown minimum was 0.861 m — the 0.049 m difference is controller lag, which is why the plan is checked against 0.90 m so the flight still clears the 0.80 m floor. Acceleration is deliberately not plotted: /tf carries float‑quantised positions, and a second difference of those is dominated by rounding.
